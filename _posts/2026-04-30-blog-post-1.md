# The Magic of $$e$$

Euler's number, denoted as **$$e$$**, is a mathematical constant approximately equal to **2.71828**. It is the base of the natural logarithm.

## Why is it special?
It is the unique number such that the value of the derivative of the function $$f(x) = e^x$$ at the point $$x = 0$$ is exactly 1. 

In calculus, this leads to the beautiful property:
$$\frac{d}{dx} e^x = e^x$$

## Compound Interest
One way to visualize $$e$$ is through the lens of compound interest. If you have $$1$$ and an interest rate of $$100%$$ per year, compounded infinitely, you end up with:
$$e = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n!}$$
